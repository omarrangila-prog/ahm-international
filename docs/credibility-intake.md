# CREDIBILITY INTAKE

Fill in what you can verify. Leave anything unverifiable blank — a blank stays
"Available on request", which is safe. A wrong entry is not.

Hand this back completed and I wire it in one pass.

**Rule:** every line has an *Evidence* field. If you cannot name the document,
leave the value blank. That field is the whole point of the system.

## TIER 1 — IDENTITY · takes credibility 4 → 8

Without these, a procurement director cannot confirm AHM is a real trading entity.
This is the single highest-value block on the whole project.

```

   "they never reply" impression that a time gap creates.)
```

## TIER 3 — THIRD-PARTY VERIFICATION · takes credibility 9 → 10

**This is the tier that actually reaches 10, and it is the one people skip.**

A self-asserted fact — however true — caps at about 8. What moves a buyer to 10 is
something they can **check without asking you**. Any one of these does more than
another page of copy:

```
CERTIFICATION (only if genuinely held):
  Standard:               ____________________________________________
  Certificate number:     ____________________________________________
  Issuing body:           ____________________________________________
  Valid until:            ____________________________________________
  (Buyers verify these against the issuer's register. A certificate
   number that does not check out is worse than no certificate.)

EXPORT / TRADE LICENCE:
  Licence or exporter no: ____________________________________________
  Issuing authority:      ____________________________________________
  Publicly searchable?    YES / NO
  (A registration a buyer can look up in a government register is the
   cheapest 10/10 credibility signal that exists. It costs nothing to
   publish and cannot be faked.)

CHAMBER OF COMMERCE / TRADE BODY MEMBERSHIP:
  Body:                   ____________________________________________
  Membership number:      ____________________________________________

NAMED REFERENCE — one customer willing to take a call:
  Company:                ____________________________________________
  Contact + role:         ____________________________________________
  WRITTEN PERMISSION HELD? YES / NO   ← must be YES to publish anything
  (Even a reference available privately on request, mentioned without
   naming them, is a stronger signal than none.)

THIRD-PARTY INSPECTION HISTORY:
  Agency used (SGS, BV, Intertek, buyer's own):  ______________________
  Willing to state that bulk is offered for third-party inspection? YES / NO
  (This is a process commitment, not an audit score. It is publishable
   without any certificate and it reassures every serious buyer.)
```

## WHAT I WILL NOT PUBLISH

Even if you fill these in, they stay hidden unless the evidence column is complete.
These are the fields that end supplier relationships when they turn out to be wrong:

- `facilitySize` — needs a verified measurement
- `employeeCount` — needs a verified headcount
- `machineCount` — needs a verified machinery list
- `monthlyCapacity` — needs production records
- `yearsInBusiness` — needs the registration date
- Any customer name or logo — needs **written** permission

---

## WHY 10/10 NEEDS TIER 3

Tier 1 proves AHM exists. Tier 2 proves AHM has done the work. Neither is
independently checkable — a buyer still has to take your word for it, and every
supplier they are evaluating says the same things.

Tier 3 is different: a licence number in a public register, a certificate the
issuer confirms, a customer who answers the phone. That is what a sourcing
director means by credible, and it is the only route to 10.

**If you supply nothing else, supply the export/trade licence number.** It is
free, public, unfakeable, and no competitor site publishes it.
