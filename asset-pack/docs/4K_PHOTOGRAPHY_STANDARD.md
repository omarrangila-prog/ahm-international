# 4K Photography / Image Generation Standard

**Visual language:** premium editorial industrial apparel photography; authentic Pakistan manufacturing; modern global sourcing company; crisp textile detail; sophisticated natural light; controlled cream/graphite/cobalt palette.

## Never use
- fake futuristic machines
- visible third-party logos
- customer POs / invoices / tech packs
- staged handshake stock photography
- AI-distorted hands or equipment
- random color grading between images
- embedded headings or marketing text

## Consistency
Keep the same:
- white balance
- contrast curve
- background tone
- lens feel
- garment proportions
- product lighting
- post-production style

## Delivery
Retain master 4K files. Build web derivatives in WebP/AVIF. Hero video should have a compressed mobile-safe version.
